    Soundboard.Tenants.Membership (soundboard v1.7.0)

    Copy Markdown

    Who may use and who may manage a guild's soundboard.

    A user may use a guild's soundboard only while they are a member of that Discord guild. The deployment's default guild (Tenants.default_guild_id/0) is open to every signed-in user, so self-hosted installs keep working with no configuration (the role check already gates who signs in there).

    Membership is read from Discord through EDA.Cache.fetch_member/2: the gateway's member cache first, the REST API on a miss. When Discord cannot be reached the answer is no.
